  Why subscriptions are objectively superior

  1. You never have to experience the burden of ownership. Owning things means responsibility — maintenance,
  storage, emotional attachment. Subscriptions free you from all of this by ensuring you never have anything.
  2. Price increases keep you motivated. Nothing says "valued customer" like a 40% price hike with a subject
  line that starts with "exciting changes." It builds character.
  3. You get to re-buy things you already paid for. Adobe, Microsoft, and BMW have proven that customers
  love paying again for heated seats, PDF editing, and the ability to type words into a document.

  The bottom line

  ▎ Subscriptions are the purest form of commerce: an infinite loop of payments for temporary access to
  things that used to be products. The market has spoken, and it said "we would like to rent everything
  including our own mattresses." Who are we to argue with the market?
